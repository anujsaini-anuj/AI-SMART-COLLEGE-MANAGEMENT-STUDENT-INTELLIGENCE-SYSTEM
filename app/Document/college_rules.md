# College Rules

## Attendance Policy
[Students must maintain at least 75% attendance to be eligible for their final examinations.]

## Library Policy
[
1. Students can borrow a maximum of 3 books from the college library at one time.

2. The library borrowing period is 14 days.

3. Students who return books late must pay a late fee of Rs. 10 per day for each overdue book.

4. Students must carry their college identity card while entering the library.
]

## Fee Policy
[
1. Students must pay their college fees according to the official fee schedule.

2. Students should contact the college accounts department for fee payment queries.
]

## Admission Policy
[ Students must contact the college admission office for admission-related queries.]

## Examination Policy
[
1. Students must maintain at least 75% attendance to be eligible to appear for their final examinations.

2. Students must carry their college identity card and examination admit card to the examination hall.

3. Students must follow all examination rules and instructions issued by the college.

4. Students must not use unauthorized materials or engage in unfair practices during examinations.

 ]


## College Office Hours
[ The college office operates Monday to Friday, from 9:00 AM to 4:00 PM.]

## Academic Queries
[Students should contact their Head of Department for department-related academic queries.]
