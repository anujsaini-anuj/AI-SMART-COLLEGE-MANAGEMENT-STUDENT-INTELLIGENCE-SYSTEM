
# College Rules

## Attendance Policy
[Students must maintain at least 75% attendance to be eligible for their final examinations.]

## Library Policy
[
1. Students can borrow a maximum of 3 books from the college library at one time.

2. The library borrowing period is 14 days.

3. Students who return books late must pay a late fee of Rs. 10 per day for each overdue book.

4. Students must carry their college identity card while entering the library.
]

## Fee Policy
[
1. Students must pay their college fees according to the official fee schedule.

2. Students should contact the college accounts department for fee payment queries.
]
